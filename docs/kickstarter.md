## HOW A READING IS MADE (this is the trust section — keep it)

Every reading is produced in three honest stages. **No artificial intelligence writes the words.** The same chart on the same day always gives the same reading, because it is derived, not invented.

**1 · You tell us when and where you began.**
Your birth date, time, and place. From these we cast your natal chart — the same details any astrologer asks for.

**2 · We calculate the sky, precisely.**
An ephemeris (the data professional astrologers rely on) fixes the Sun, Moon, and planets to a fraction of a degree — your chart, and each day's transits to it. Moon phase, retrogrades, the day's ruling transit.

**3 · We read it into words, by tradition.**
Tables of correspondence — the meanings tradition gives each planet, sign, house, and aspect — turn the day's strongest transit into a single, warm line. Every word traces back to a rule and a source.

## FAQ

**Is this fortune-telling?**
No. It never predicts events or gives advice. It's a calculated, tradition-based reflection for the day.

**Do I need an app or Wi-Fi?**
No. It runs fully offline. It only needs power.

**How is it different from a horoscope app?**
Apps show the same generic sun-sign text to millions and mine your attention. Almanac Stone casts _your_ chart, shows one honest line, and asks nothing back.

**What if I don't know my exact birth time?**
It still works. We cast a noon chart and leave out the houses, and we tell you so.

**Is the astrology "real"?**
The astronomy is literally true and verifiable. The interpretation follows a named, respected tradition, reviewed by a practicing astrologer. We're transparent about exactly how it's done.
